Frequently Asked Questions:

How do I create an account on the job portal?

To create an account, click on the "Sign Up" button and fill in your details such as name, email address, and password. You may also be asked to upload a resume and complete your profile by adding your work experience, skills, and educational background.

How can I search for job openings?

Once logged in, you can search for jobs by entering relevant keywords, such as job title, industry, or location, in the search bar. You can also filter results by salary range, job type (e.g., full-time, part-time), and company.

How do I apply for a job?

After finding a job you're interested in, click on the job listing to view details. If you meet the requirements, click the "Apply Now" button, and either upload your resume or use the resume already saved in your profile.

What should I do if I forget my password?

If you've forgotten your password, click the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page. You'll be prompted to enter your registered email address, and a password reset link will be sent to your email.
